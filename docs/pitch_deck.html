03. Innovation Patient Safety

Real-Time Contraindication Sentinel

EchoDoc doesn't just record words—it actively guards against critical clinical pharmacology errors while the conversation is happening.

Example: WARFARIN + IBUPROFEN DETECTED CRITICAL SEVERITY

Risk: Severe gastrointestinal hemorrhage. Concomitant use of Warfarin with NSAIDs impairs platelet aggregation and damages gastric mucosa.

Action Taken: Visual alert banner triggers immediately; Voice Copilot tool verifies drug interaction and audibly informs the clinician before prescription sign-off.
